# Invoice-bound reconciliation evidence — staging only

This transaction-owned seam checks a persisted paid sale header, service details and exact payment amounts against an internally supplied membership settlement proposal, plus tenant client/catalog scope and trial/approval historical pins. It appends one invoice-keyed evidence row and optional void evidence after a cancellation removes the header. No separate commit or transaction is created; the caller owns it. The records are evidence only and are not an entitlement, a count of actual membership capacity, or a financial posting. They are intentionally separate from synthetic receipt usage. No actual invoice create/edit/delete call sites are wired; current prices, active proof/approval and actual usage must still be checked in the sale transaction before creating an invoice. Void evidence is not proof of cash/stock reversal, and the caller must verify those. Zero-total invoices and bonus coins remain excluded. Tenant/branch/login/feature guard and no production migration. Legacy invoice identity is the sales namespace invoice ID; do not reuse it after void.
