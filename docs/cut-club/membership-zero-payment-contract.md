# Zero-payment membership invoice contract (preview only)

A zero-total invoice is a completed service invoice, not a payment. The pure plan returns NO_PAYMENT, null header method, no payment rows, no cash movement, no clearing redistribution. Positive totals require exact minor-unit allocation, distinct authorized payment methods and no submitted clearing method. Zero totals remain default-denied unless explicitly requested in a simulation. This module is not wired into POST /api/sales or the invoice writers.

Actual integration prerequisites discovered in current code: both legacy route and extracted sale transaction require a header PaymentMethodID, the legacy route relies on InsCashMoveSales to post an initial cash move, and the extracted path may post through the treasury port. Confirm nullability and trigger behavior in the real staging schema, add a non-cash sale classification only after accounting review, verify reports/commissions/loyalty/stock, and test no cash movement plus full transactional cancellation. Never use a fictitious one-piastre payment, cash method or clearing method to bypass validation. The current live POS remains unchanged and zero-total sales remain blocked.
