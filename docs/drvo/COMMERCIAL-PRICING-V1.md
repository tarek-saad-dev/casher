# DRVOERP V1 — Commercial Pricing

> **Implementation snapshot, not the finalized business model (2026-10-26).** 
> See **[DRVO ERP — Business Model & Pricing Strategy](./BUSINESS-MODEL-V1.md)** for the approved commercial direction and **[Salon Pricing & Feature Matrix](./SALON-PRICING-FEATURE-MATRIX-V1.md)** for the *proposed, unimplemented* feature packaging and validation gates. 
> The prices/capacities below document the **existing V1 code/catalog** only. They are **not yet** the approved final market offer and do not imply the future feature matrix is implemented.

Effective: 2026-10-11

## Public plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Capacity |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starter | 499 EGP | 4,990 EGP | 1 branch / 5 users |
| Growth | 899 EGP | 8,990 EGP | 3 branches / 20 users |
| Pro | 1,499 EGP | 14,990 EGP | 10 branches / 75 users |

Annual billing is priced as 10 monthly payments (2 months free).

All public plans keep the same product architecture: the industry pack and tenant app composition determine which apps are installed. Plans control commercial capacity and subscription state, not feature flags.

## V1 sales policy

- 14-day free trial.
- Billing is operator-managed; no payment gateway is required for V1.
- Growth is the default recommendation for a normal multi-employee salon.
- Guided onboarding/data entry can be sold as a one-time 1,500 EGP service.
- Legacy-data migration is quoted case by case.
- WhatsApp + AI Receptionist is not sold to a new tenant until per-tenant WhatsApp channel connection is commercially ready.

## Positioning

Starter: small single-branch salons that want to move operations onto one system.

Growth: the default offer for established salons with a team and room to grow.

Pro: larger operations and multi-branch groups.
